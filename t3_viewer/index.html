2D art from the disc: portraits, title and menu screens, HUD fonts, Theater stills. Most of it is LZ-compressed; colour 0 is transparent, as on the PS1. Screen-sized pictures are assembled from their strips. Produced by t3_converter/src/export_pictures.rs.

The system sounds (hits, menus) and announcer banks embedded in the EXE, plus each character's VAB sound bank, decoded from PS1 SPU ADPCM and played at the rate the game keys them (key = tone + 60: about 16 kHz for system sounds, 8 kHz for voices).

The 50 XA-ADPCM streams the game indexes in its EXE (37.8 kHz stereo), plus the CD audio track. Track names aren't stored on the disc.

The 22 STR movies (MDEC video + XA audio), as Motion-JPEG frames (AVI) and a FLAC soundtrack. Titles come from watching them.

Arenas are produced by t3_converter/src/export_stages.rs. They are drawn unlit with the stage's vertex colours, like on the PS1. Smooth textures and wireframe apply here too.

Plain glTF 2.0 player (GLTFLoader + AnimationMixer). Models are produced by t3_converter/src/export_gltf.rs from your own disc; the game's IK is already baked into the animations. Drop any .glb onto the page to view it.
